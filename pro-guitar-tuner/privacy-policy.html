Privacy Policy

Legend Guitar Tuner (Android) · Effective 29 October 2026

Short version: tuning happens entirely on your device and your audio never leaves it. The app has no accounts. If you choose to watch an ad to unlock a tuning, Google’s ad service receives device information to show that ad. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone permission so it can hear your guitar and measure its pitch. Audio is analysed in real time, in memory, on your device. It is never recorded, saved to a file or sent anywhere. The microphone is used only while the tuner screen is open, and you can revoke the permission at any time in your device settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Standard tuning and your own custom tunings are free. Other tunings can be unlocked by choosing to watch a rewarded ad, which is provided by Google AdMob. Ads are shown only when you ask to unlock a tuning. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address (from which approximate location can be inferred), device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Google settings. Pro subscribers see no ads.

Subscriptions (Google Play)

The optional Pro subscription is sold and processed by Google Play. Payment details go to Google, not to us; the app only learns whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your settings (such as reference pitch and theme), your custom tunings, favourites and recently used tunings in its private storage on your device. This data stays on your device. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account, under Google’s terms; you control this in your device settings. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

What we don’t do

We don’t run our own servers, analytics or tracking, we don’t sell data, and we don’t receive your name, email or payment details. Internet access is used only for ads and Google Play purchases; tuning itself works offline.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed to collect personal information from them.
