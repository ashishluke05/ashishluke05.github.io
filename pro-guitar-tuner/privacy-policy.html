Privacy Policy

Pro Guitar Tuner (Android) · Effective 29 October 2026

Short version: Pro Guitar Tuner works entirely on your device. It does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no internet access.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone permission so it can hear your guitar and measure its pitch. Audio is analysed in real time, in memory, on your device. It is never recorded, saved to a file or sent anywhere. The microphone is used only while the tuner screen is open, and you can revoke the permission at any time in your device settings.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your settings (such as reference pitch and theme), your custom tunings, favourites and recently used tunings in its private storage on your device. This data stays on your device. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account, under Google’s terms; you control this in your device settings. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

No tracking, ads or network access

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and does not request internet access, so it cannot send data off your device.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
